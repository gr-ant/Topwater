Terminal demo

Recording viewer

Step through a recorded agent session turn by turn: each command and what it returned, the machine's files as they were after that turn, and what the turn changed, or compare any two moments. From any turn you can continue in a terminal on that machine: what you type becomes a branch of the timeline, which you can compare with the original and download as its own .topwater file.

Recordings are the .topwater files Topwater writes (shell.StartRecording() in .NET, startRecording(shell) in TypeScript, shell.start_recording() in Python); older JSON Lines recordings open too. Everything happens in this tab; nothing is uploaded.

Or drop a .topwater file anywhere on this page (an old .jsonl recording together with its .zip snapshots, if it keeps them in separate files).
